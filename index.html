What do I need to bring to pick up my order?

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. The name must match your order.

How fast is in-store pickup?

Most orders are packed and ready in about 20 minutes. You get a text when your order is ready.

Are your products lab tested?

Every batch is tested by a state-licensed lab for potency, pesticides, residual solvents, heavy metals and microbials. The certificate of analysis is linked on each product.

Do you deliver?

Yes, same-day delivery is available within our service area. Enter your ZIP code above to check availability and the minimum order.

What is the difference between indica, sativa and hybrid?

They describe a strain's plant family and are a rough guide to how it tends to feel. Many people find indicas like Granddaddy Purple more relaxing, sativas like Sour Diesel more energizing, and hybrids like Blue Dream in between. Terpenes and your own body matter too, so ask a budtender if you're unsure.

How much can I buy at once?

State law sets daily purchase limits for adult-use customers, such as 28.5 grams of flower or 8 grams of concentrate in California. Our checkout warns you before you go over.
